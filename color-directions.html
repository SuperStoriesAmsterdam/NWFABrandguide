six ways to make green & gold cool

Same poster, same layout, same content — six colour directions. In every one: the wine bottle stays the hero, the festival name is set in Inter (calm, lowercase), and Vicky appears only on the payoff “united by wine”. That's the agreed structure; what changes here is purely the colour world and how the bottle is drawn.

The thread through all of them: a warm paper ground instead of full-bleed colour, desaturated hues, and colour used as accent, not wallpaper. That's what turns "loud" into "cool." Pick the ones that pull you — we can also merge two.
