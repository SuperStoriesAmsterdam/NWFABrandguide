workshops are booked after you've purchased your festival ticket. you'll receive a reservation link within 24 hours to claim a seat, one workshop per pass, first come first served. more workshops will be added over the coming months. buy a ticket and reserve your workshop. changing your workshop is possible until 28 february, 11.00.
