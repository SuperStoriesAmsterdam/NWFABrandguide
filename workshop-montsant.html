§ what it is

schist, slate,
and stubborn old vines.

montsant is the quieter sister to priorat — same poor, mineral soils, same old-vine grenache and carignan, but worked by smaller hands. patri pours six wines straight from the farm and walks you through what the geology does to the glass.

this is a tasting masterclass, not a lecture. expect questions, side-tangents, and one or two bottles you'd never have ordered yourself.

§ the host

patri morillo.

celler comunica · catalonia, es

patri runs celler comunica with her partner, a tiny project in catalonia making honest, low-intervention wines from old garnacha and carignan vines. she farms organically with wild ferments — no shortcuts, no makeup. one of the most articulate guides to the region you'll find pouring outside spain.
