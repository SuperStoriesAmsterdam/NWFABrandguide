the festival

natural wine festival a'dam is, for the netherlands, a completely unique wine event. on sunday 28 february we open the doors to consumers and professionals, and on monday 1 march to the trade.

after a sold-out pilot in 2022 and four editions since, we host the fifth in the heart of amsterdam, at de hallen, in the hallen studio's and denim city.

curated natural winemakers from across europe, an extensive workshop programme, a pop-up restaurant with carefully paired food, and a mini market. NWFA is a love child gone wild. let's share the love.

what we celebrate

winemakers,
in person.

the festival celebrates wines made without pesticides, without herbicides, and with as little added sulphite as possible. wines produced by incredibly skilled farmers, with a story to tell about agriculture that can regenerate.

we share these wines, and some of the knowledge of the people who make them. you talk to the source directly. no dumb questions, since the maker knows their ground better than any sommelier.

the stance

beyond the hype.

some still call these cloudy, unfiltered wines "dirty". for others they add up to a passing hype.

below the hype there is a deeper story: tradition married with innovation, craft meeting foresight. nothing taken out, nothing added beyond a tiny dose of sulphite at bottling. living wines that reflect their terroir and fruit.

these wines are simply common sense. and sometimes common sense takes the single-mindedness of a visionary. or a rebel.
