28 feb & 1 mar ’27 · de hallen, amsterdam

Dear {firstName},

Five years ago, we launched the Natural Wine Festival Amsterdam. We’ve learned a lot along the way: building, tweaking, and constantly striving to make it better. Two years ago, we added a dedicated second day for professionals, but November never felt like the right window.

For years, spring dates weren’t an option at De Hallen Studios (our partners without whom NWFA wouldn’t exist) due to a weekly radio production. But during a recent conversation with an Italian distributor, the idea came up again. We reached out to De Hallen, and as it turned out, the space had just freed up. We got an instant green light.

We are officially moving the NWFA to Feb 28th & March 1st, 2027.

Our goal has always been to build an event that goes beyond the hype. In major cities, natural wine has become a staple; outside of them, it’s still widely undiscovered. That means there are still endless stories, producers, and wines waiting to be shared.

NWFA remains deeply rooted in the farming and the wines you represent: innovative, hospitable, and accessible, yet high-end.
