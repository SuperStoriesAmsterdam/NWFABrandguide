01 · brand overview

one festival, one look

The festival is a two-day celebration of natural wine culture: winemakers, food, workshops, tastings and community. The identity sits on a warm cream surface with a deep forest-green dark layer. Type is lowercase Figtree. The marks are hand-printed woodcut icons in navy, gold and green, and a pixel-bead (ministeck) language for poster and large-format use.

rules

  • Paper is the dominant surface. Ink is the dark layer.
  • Navy appears inside the marks. Posts use no navy background, except the hero variant and the ticket-sales post, on --navy-deep.
  • Green is text on paper (the payoff) and the fill inside icons. It is never a background.
  • The payoff on dark panels is amber. Measured contrast of amber on ink is 7.58.
  • The three golds differ and may keep differing: woodcut icons use #dbbb00, the ministeck beads sit near #f0b000 (approximate), the site and posts use amber #eeba3f.
  • Flat colour. The one gradient is the ministeck glow, on screen.
  • On the website a pill call to action is fine. Social posts use no pills: the call to action sits on a brush stroke.

Set lowercase. Used for the payoff and for the descriptor line under an award name, such as "best under 30 contemporary sommelier" under "the natural". Never used for headlines. 05 · icons

hand-printed, always the same hand

Woodcut icons: bold outlines, flat fills, rough edges. No clean vector, no gradients, no 3D. They do the wayfinding on the site and in social posts. Round badges sit inside a circle with a navy border. Icons stay at 48px or larger. Labels under icons are lowercase Figtree. New icons match the woodcut style; a third illustration style is not added.

round icons · site set (icons-set/)

venue icons · optional church windows

The church-window emblems tie an activity to the venue, De Hallen. They stay in the system as an optional layer and are used where a post or page gives the venue space to show. The originals are in icons-/ and the social versions in concept-assets/ (win-*).

06 · ministeck marks

the form-language of edition 05

Navy beads on a gold field, on a visible grid, with a soft gold-to-green glow and a few gold sparkle beads. Exported at 256 colours so the glow stays clean. The same marks serve as square app icons for Good Juice (by NWFA). Each mark exists at 1024, 512, 256, 128 and 64px, as PNG, in logos-/goodjuice-marks/. The grape cluster, the hero image of edition 05, is rebuilt from its bead grid as a vector (SVG) and a 6000px print master for poster and large-format use.

Ministeck is the form-language of edition 05: navy beads on gold, with a little green. It is used at poster and large-format size, and the grape cluster is the hero image. Social posts use the woodcut icons, which rhyme with the beads: the same navy, gold and green, the same grid, the same silhouettes.

07 · the social system

one system, the register leads

Posts are 1080 × 1080 (also 1080 × 1350 for 4:5); stories are 1080 × 1920. The maker register leads: a label with region and country, a framed photo, a name. The manifesto, countdown and ticket posts are post-types inside the same system, with the same grid and the same logic. The lead image of the edition is the grape cluster in ministeck.

Posts on a flat ground carry an inner frame: inset 4cqw, border .5cqw, in ink on paper and in cream on dark panels, with 5.5cqw padding inside. The call to action in social posts is an amber stroke with a darker copy shifted a few pixels down and right, rotated -1.5°, text in ink, Figtree 700. The stroke is one closed shape and nothing crosses the text.

09 · photography

documentary and warm

Event photography is documentary and warm. Real people, real wine, real moments. Never staged.

subjects

  • People tasting, talking, laughing
  • Wine glasses and bottles in use, uncomposed
  • Festival atmosphere: crowds, light, movement
  • Food preparation, market stalls
  • Winemakers in vineyards (for winemaker profiles)

treatment

  • Photos are used at full saturation: no desaturation, no filters
  • On social templates photos sit in a bordered frame: an ink border on paper posts and a cream border on dark posts
  • Photos are never overlaid with color tints
  • Text over photos sits on a dark scrim gradient that starts transparent and ends at the text, as in the ticket post
  • Photo-heavy layouts alternate with illustration-heavy layouts
  • Only photography from the festival and its winemakers is used

file naming

{context}-{subject}-{number}.{ext} event-tasting-01.jpg · event-crowd-01.jpg · winemaker-portrait-01.jpg food-preparation-01.jpg · venue-interior-01.jpg

The existing files in photography-/ (91) carry camera names such as HOI_1536.jpg. The naming convention applies to new files.
