# BrandguideNWFA: design source of truth

`BRANDGUIDE.md` is the only design reference for this repo. The guide page `brandguide/index.html` and the reference file `social/social-system.html` follow it.

## Rule for every session

- Read `BRANDGUIDE.md` before any design work: social posts, pages, icons, marks, colour or type changes.
- When a task needs a decision the guide does not cover, ask Peter. Once he decides, update `BRANDGUIDE.md` in the same change, so the guide stays the source of truth.
- A rule from an older file, an old commit or an old chat does not count when it differs from `BRANDGUIDE.md`.

## The rules that matter most

- Colour roles: paper is the surface, ink `#1B3326` is the dark layer, amber `#eeba3f` is calls to action and highlights, ochre is labels, green is payoff text on paper and fill inside icons. Navy is for the marks only. The deep navy `#131f4a` ground exists for the hero and ticket-sales posts only.
- Type: Figtree for display and body, lowercase headlines. Vicky Black only as the payoff ("united by wine") and as the descriptor line under an award name ("best under 30 contemporary sommelier"). No serif.
- The name is always "natural wine festival a'dam".
- No pills in social posts: the call to action sits on the brush stroke. Pills are fine on the website.
- Ministeck is for poster and large-format use. Social posts use the woodcut icons.
- The hero post proportions are in `BRANDGUIDE.md` section 07. Posts built on that layout use those numbers.
