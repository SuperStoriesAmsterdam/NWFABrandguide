§ 04 — philosophy

building success together, so everybody wins.

Participation fees are kept low, with the option for winemakers to earn back part of their fee through direct bottle sales at NWFA.

Distributors and winemakers are encouraged to promote their attendance via mailing lists and social media. NWFA provides creative assets to support outreach.

transparency

Non-distributed winemakers are asked to provide a chemical analysis of wines. Sulfur levels and farming practices (organic, biodynamic, etc.) will be published on winemaker profiles on the NWFA website.

zero-zero wines

Zero-zero wines are highlighted with a special label.

selection criteria

wines must reflect terroir and the hand of the winemaker.

  • ✦Organic, biodynamic, or regenerative vineyard work (certification not mandatory).
  • ✦No use of pesticides or herbicides.
  • ✦Indigenous yeast fermentation.
  • ✦No acidification, no chaptalization, no artificial adjustments.
  • ✦No or only light filtering/fining (always vegan).
  • ✦No or low sulfur; added only at bottling.
